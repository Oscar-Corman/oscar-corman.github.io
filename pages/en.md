relational work / bodywork / self-awareness / nature-based practices

i work with body-oriented and mindfulness-based methods to support people in their personal growth. complemented by conversation, working through breath, movement, and voice forms the core and most effective part of my approach. depending on your preference, sessions can take place with or without direct touch and physical contact.

drawing on insights from health sciences, neuroscience, philosophy, and spiritual practice, i support you in navigating challenging situations and complex life transitions.

a biopsychosocial understanding of health helps take into account the interplay between body, mind, and relationships throughout processes of change.

the aim is to strengthen self-worth, self-knowledge, and embodied confidence. together, we explore what might lie at the root of an issue and what potential paths forward could look like—while all decisions ultimately remain yours.
